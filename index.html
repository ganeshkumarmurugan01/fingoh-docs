Getting started

What is Fingoh?

Fingoh is a B2B trade fair exhibitor intelligence platform. It helps exhibitors identify, score, and engage high-intent visitors before, during, and after an event.

Fingoh combines AI-powered profile enrichment, XGBoost intent scoring, and on-site signal capture to build a ranked visitor list that tells your sales team exactly who to prioritise and why.

How it works

  • 1
    Upload your visitor list
    Import a CSV or Excel export from your CRM, event platform, or organiser backend.
  • 2
    Fingoh enriches and scores
    Each contact is enriched using Claude AI and scored across 41 signals using the XGBoost IEI model. Results are ready in 20–60 seconds per batch.
  • 3
    Review IEI tiers
    Visitors are ranked T1 (Hot) through T4 (Cold). Drill into any visitor's full intelligence profile.
  • 4
    Request meetings
    Send personalised meeting requests. Visitors accept or decline via email. Confirmed meetings appear in the Staff App.
  • 5
    Log on-site signals
    Booth staff use the Staff App PWA to log conversation quality, questions, behaviour signals, and walk-in visitors in real time.
  • 6
    Export and follow up
    After the event, export ranked leads as CSV. The Fingoh Agent drafts personalised follow-up sequences for every T1/T2 lead.

Key concepts

Understanding these concepts will help you get the most out of Fingoh.

IEI — Investigative Event Intelligence

IEI is Fingoh's proprietary intent scoring framework. It produces a score from 0–100 for each visitor by combining 41 weighted signals. The higher the score, the higher the likelihood of conversion.

IEI Tiers

TierLabelScoreWhat it means
T1Hot≥ 53Active buyer, strong ICP fit. Prioritise immediately.
T2Warm43–52Evaluating options. Follow up within 48 hours.
T3Cool36–42General interest. Nurture with content.
T4Cold< 36Low intent or poor ICP match. Low priority.

ICP — Ideal Customer Profile

Your definition of the perfect buyer for this event — including target roles, company sizes, visit reasons, and product categories. Configured in Event Setup and used to calibrate all AI enrichment and scoring.

✅
Better ICP = better scores
Always complete Event Setup before uploading your audience.
Getting started

Quick start

Get your first event live in under 10 minutes.

  • 1
    Sign in at exhibitor.fingoh.ai
    Use your registered email address.
  • 2
    Create or select an event
    Click + New event. Enter the event name, venue, and dates.
  • 3
    Complete Event Setup
    Fill in your ICP — target roles, company sizes, visit reasons, and categories. Takes 3–5 minutes.
  • 4
    Upload your audience
    Go to Audience Upload and drop your CSV or Excel file. Required: first_name, last_name, email, company, job_title.
  • 5
    Review IEI Analysis
    Once scoring completes (20–60 seconds), go to IEI Analysis to see your ranked visitor list.
  • 6
    Add your booth staff
    Go to Event Setup → Staff and add your team's email addresses.

Glossary

Key terms used throughout the Fingoh platform.

IEI — Investigative Event Intelligence
Fingoh's proprietary intent scoring framework. Produces a 0–100 score for each visitor by combining 41 weighted signals across pre-event, on-site, and post-event data phases.
Scoring
IEI Tier (T1–T4)
Classification based on IEI score: T1 Hot (≥53), T2 Warm (43–52), T3 Cool (36–42), T4 Cold (<36). Used to prioritise which visitors to approach first.
Scoring
ICP — Ideal Customer Profile
Your definition of the perfect buyer for this event — target roles, company sizes, industries, visit reasons, and product categories. Configured per event in Event Setup.
Setup
XGBoost
The gradient-boosted tree model that produces IEI scores. Trained across 41 signal features. Deployed on Modal as a serverless endpoint with separate dev and prod deployments.
Technology
Claude enrichment
The AI step that estimates 9 signal values per visitor (seniority, ICP fit, company size match, buying cycle stage, etc.) before XGBoost scoring.
Technology
Deep IEI
Enhanced intelligence pass that runs web research on a visitor — LinkedIn, company news, funding rounds — and enriches their intelligence layers. Costs one IEI credit per contact.
Feature
On-site IEI
The updated IEI score after staff log conversation signals. Combines pre-event IEI with on-site behaviour signals from the Staff App.
Scoring
Walk-in
A visitor who arrives at the booth but is not in the pre-registered list. Captured via the Staff App using business card scan or manual entry.
Feature
Meeting match score
A score predicting how well a visitor's profile aligns with your event goals for a meeting. Computed by the LambdaMART meeting scorer.
Scoring
PWA — Progressive Web App
The Staff App is a PWA — install it to your phone's home screen from the browser. No App Store required. Works offline. Supported on Chrome (Android) and Safari (iOS).
Technology
IEI credits
Credits consumed when running Deep IEI. Each plan includes: Free Trial 10, Starter 50, Growth 200, Scale 1,000.

Frequently asked questions

How long does scoring take?

For a 100-row CSV, scoring typically completes in 60–90 seconds. Larger uploads may take 3–5 minutes. Scoring runs in the background — you can leave the page and return.

Can I upload the same contact twice?

Yes. Fingoh uses email as the unique key. Re-uploading a contact updates their record and re-runs scoring.

What if a visitor's email bounces?

You'll see an error in the meeting status. Update the visitor's email in their profile and resend the request.

How do staff log in without a smartphone?

The Staff App works on any device with a browser — laptop, tablet, or phone. Open staff.fingoh.ai in Chrome or Safari.

What happens to offline-queued signals?

Signals logged offline are stored in the browser's local storage and synced automatically when the device reconnects. Tap "Sync now" to force an immediate sync.

Can multiple staff log signals for the same visitor?

Yes. Each signal is attributed to the staff member who logged it. The on-site IEI score uses the most recent signal data.

Where do I get support?

Email us at hello@fingoh.ai. We typically respond within 4 business hours.
